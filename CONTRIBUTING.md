# Contribution Guidelines

Contributions are welcome! To keep **Awesome Barter** clean, open, and reliable, please follow these minimal guidelines when opening a Pull Request:

## Eligibility & Quality Rules

1. **Strictly Open-Source & Public:** Submitted software must be open-source under a recognized license (e.g., MIT, Apache-2.0, GPL, etc.) and hosted in a public source repository. Closed-source apps, commercial landing pages, and URL shorteners are not accepted.
2. **Active Maintenance (1-Year Rule):** Repositories must show at least one commit or release within the **last 12 months**. Repositories inactive for over 1 year will be pruned during periodic reviews (this does not apply to completed books, academic papers, or static field manuals).
3. **Repository Health:** Projects with broken links, unaddressed critical issues, spam, or reported security risks will be rejected or removed upon review/report.
4. **Formatting & Neutrality:**
   * Add your link in **alphabetical order** within the appropriate category.
   * Use the exact format: `* [Project Name](https://link) - Concise, neutral description.`
   * Avoid marketing buzzwords or subjective claims (e.g., "the best", "revolutionary"). Describe strictly what the tool does.
   * Submit one project per Pull Request.

## Reporting Broken or Unsafe Links
If you spot an abandoned repository, broken link, or malicious project on this list, please open an **Issue** so it can be reviewed and removed.
